Q3 2026: the dataset and the figures

The Mahler Sotheby’s International Realty Door County report records 206 closed single-family home and condo sales, a rounded median sale price of $497,000 and 232 active properties at quarter end. The data period ends September 30, 2026; the report was updated October 6, 2026.

The publisher’s price distribution includes 79 sales from $250,000 to $500,000 and 52 above $750,000. This describes the reported quarter and property scope. It is not a live inventory count, a report on land or commercial property, or proof that every community follows the same pattern.

Asking prices, sold prices and inventory are different

A listing’s asking price is not a closed sale price. Quarter-end inventory is a snapshot, while closed sales cover a period. Avoid combining these measures or comparing different time windows without explaining what changed.

County summaries also combine property locations and types. Fish Creek’s publisher-defined report is a narrower dataset with a smaller sample; neither dataset should be substituted for a property-specific comparable-sale review.

What this report does not forecast

A change in median price does not measure the appreciation of every home. Shifts in the properties sold can affect both median and average. No universal selling timeframe, current absorption rate, days-on-market figure or future-price forecast is asserted here.

Ask Tom for a dated analysis of the actual area and property type when planning an offer or listing. Keep the source, period, geography and sample count with the numbers so later updates remain comparable.

Questions worth answering

Can I use the $497,000 median to price my house?

Not by itself. The median describes the quarter’s combined sales, not your property’s condition, rights, location or relevant comparable sales.

Are the 232 active properties available today?

Not necessarily. That is the source report’s September 30, 2026 quarter-end snapshot; confirm current availability through the existing property search.
