Dated market evidence · Q3 2026

Fish Creek Real Estate Market Report

Dated evidence is more useful than an undated market claim. This report summarizes the publisher-defined Fish Creek area for single-family homes and condos, not a custom Town of Gibraltar boundary extract.

Tom Reynolds website · Updated November 8, 2026

What the Q3 2026 report measures

Mahler Sotheby’s International Realty’s report shows 19 closed sales, a rounded median sale price of $616,000, a rounded average sale price of $854,000 and 30 active properties at quarter end. The underlying MLS period ends September 30, 2026; the publisher updated the report October 6, 2026.

These are historical quarter figures for the publisher’s geography and combined single-family/condo scope. They are not today’s inventory, a separate resort-condo analysis, a land report or a valuation of a particular home.

Read the five-year table with the sample size

The table below compares the same quarter in each year as reported by the publisher. In 2026 the median is lower than in 2025 while the average is higher. That can happen when the mix of properties changes; it does not establish what the same house gained or lost in value.

With 19 closings in the latest quarter and smaller prior samples, one or a few sales can materially affect summary measures. Do not convert a change in median price into an appraisal adjustment for every Fish Creek property.

Use the report to ask a better pricing question

For a purchase or sale, request comparable properties with similar location, ownership rights, condition and type. Residential condos, hotel/resort interests, waterfront and inland homes should be separated where the evidence supports a useful comparison.

No monthly days-on-market, price-per-square-foot, forecast or live pending count is invented here. Ask for a dated, licensed analysis with a consistent area definition and sample count when those measures are needed.

Questions worth answering

Did every Fish Creek home lose value because the median fell?

No. A median of different sold properties is not a repeat-sales index or an appraisal of the same home.

Does this report use the exact Town of Gibraltar boundary?

No. It uses the publisher-defined Fish Creek geography. Confirm geographic definitions before comparing it with another report.
