Make the first inquiry useful

For a purchase, share area, property type, approximate budget, timing, intended use and any listing links. For a sale, start with the address, property type and your timeline. For a condo or rental-use question, include the association or property of interest and the use you are considering.

Do not include financial account details, identification documents or other sensitive records in a general website inquiry. Ask how any documents needed for a transaction should be shared.

Ask a specific ownership question

Tom can help organize the next steps and identify where property-specific verification is needed. Legal rights, permitting, insurance, lending and technical condition questions may require the relevant authority or a qualified professional.

If you are researching first, browse the community guides and ownership resources without making an inquiry. When ready, send the relevant page or listing so the conversation starts with context.
