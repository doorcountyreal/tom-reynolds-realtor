What a useful price comparison should explain

Ask which recent sales and current competing listings were considered, how the area was defined and why the properties are comparable. Waterfront rights, condo structure, acreage, condition and improvements can make a simple price-per-square-foot comparison misleading.

Fish Creek, Sturgeon Bay, Sister Bay and other familiar place names may be defined differently in market datasets and postal addresses. Confirm the geography of any report before applying it to the property. A small sample can change substantially when the mix of sales changes.

Market analysis, estimate and appraisal are different

A comparative market analysis is a real-estate pricing tool, not a promise of a sale price. An automated estimate depends on the information and method available to its provider. An appraisal has a different professional purpose and process; ask the relevant lender or appraiser when one is required.

Review pricing alongside preparation costs, timing, competition and estimated net proceeds. Update the analysis when the market or property changes rather than treating a single number as permanent.
