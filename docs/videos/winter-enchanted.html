After the film: rehearse a winter arrival

Think about the drive, parking, entry and systems at the actual property. Who maintains access, and who can check the home if your plans change? A seasonal atmosphere can help you imagine the setting; it does not establish how a particular home performs.

Turn the impression into a property question

Ask which parts of the year-round care plan you want to handle and which need a provider. Use the ownership checklist below to compare homes by the same practical categories, then bring unresolved questions into professional due diligence.
