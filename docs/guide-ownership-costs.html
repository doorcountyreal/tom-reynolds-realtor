Door County Home Ownership Cost Worksheet

Use your own assumptions to compare two properties consistently. An ownership budget should show the costs that are known and leave room for the ones still being investigated.

Tom Reynolds website · Updated November 8, 2026

Separate recurring costs and reserves

Collect property-specific estimates for taxes, insurance, utilities, association costs and management if used. Include an allowance for maintenance and a separate reserve for larger replacements. Ask which costs an association covers so the same expense is not counted twice.

The worksheet below starts at zero and runs in your browser. It is not a quote, appraisal, tax estimate or financing offer. It does not send entries to Tom or store them on a server. Keep any private financial details out of a general inquiry.

Financing is a separate cash-flow decision

Enter annual debt payments separately so the difference between operating ownership cost and cash required after financing remains visible. The simple worksheet does not calculate amortization, interest deductions, opportunity cost or income taxes.

For rental scenarios, gross rent is not profit. Vacancy, management, operating costs, maintenance and debt payments must be considered, and legal rental eligibility has to be checked first. This worksheet deliberately avoids invented occupancy or revenue assumptions.

Use the unresolved questions in the next conversation

If a figure is unknown, obtain an estimate from the appropriate source rather than treating zero as evidence that the expense does not exist. Verify insurance, utility service and association obligations for the actual property and intended use.

Use the result to organize comparisons and ask better questions. A property-specific decision still needs documents, current quotes and advice from the relevant professionals.

Questions worth answering

Does the worksheet recommend a rental income assumption?

No. It models ownership expenses only. Rental permission and any revenue scenario need separate property-specific investigation.

Are my worksheet entries submitted anywhere?

No. The calculation takes place locally in your browser and has no submit action or server storage.
