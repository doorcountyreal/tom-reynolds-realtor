Find your place in Door County

Compare the setting, the ownership responsibilities and the way you want to spend your time. Start with a local guide, then confirm the municipality for the actual property.

Northern Door · Green Bay side

Fish Creek

A Fish Creek home can put shops, performances and the shoreline close to your everyday routine. The useful question is how much of that activity you want at your doorstep, and how the property will work outside the season that first brought you here.

Central Door · City and surrounding town

Sturgeon Bay

Sturgeon Bay brings a working waterfront, city services and Door County’s maritime identity into the property search. Start by separating the City of Sturgeon Bay from the nearby Town of Sturgeon Bay, then compare the home’s actual setting and daily routes.

Northern Door · Village waterfront

Sister Bay

Sister Bay’s waterfront, dining and village activity can be central to the appeal of a property. A strong search looks beyond proximity on a map to the way a home, parking space, association and maintenance plan fit your intended use.

Northern Door · Historic village

Ephraim

Ephraim’s historic buildings and harbor setting give a property search a distinct character. Before imagining a renovation or a new ownership routine, understand the existing structure, the applicable district and the work the property may need.

Know which office to ask

Every Door County municipality

Door County’s official directory lists one city, four villages and fourteen towns. A postal address or familiar community name does not establish the parcel’s governing office. Egg Harbor, Forestville and Sturgeon Bay each have separate municipal entities sharing a name.

Use this directory to orient your research, then verify the parcel and intended use with the responsible municipality. A linked property guide covers the broader search area; it does not replace municipal guidance.
