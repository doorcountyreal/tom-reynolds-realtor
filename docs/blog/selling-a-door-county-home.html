Preparing a Door County home for a thoughtful sale.

A strong seller plan starts long before the first showing. It gives buyers enough clarity to see the opportunity and gives you a workable path to launch.

Collect the property story

Gather the details only an owner can provide: improvement history, service records, utility information, seasonal routines, property features, and the practical things a new owner will need to understand. This is useful preparation, not a substitute for required disclosures.

Use market context, not a single number

A pricing conversation should compare current alternatives, relevant recent sales, condition, location, and the characteristics that make a Door County property distinct. A useful opinion of value is clear about both its evidence and its limits.

Decide what needs attention

Not every project creates value. Prioritize safety, deferred maintenance, obvious first impressions, and presentation details that help a buyer understand how the property lives. Consider the timing, cost, and likely benefit before beginning work.

A useful first question: What would a careful buyer need to understand quickly in order to take the next step with confidence?

Plan the launch

Good marketing follows good preparation. The right presentation, photography, property information, and showing plan should match the home and the people most likely to value it.
