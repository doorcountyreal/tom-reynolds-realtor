Door County Roots. Proven Results.

Tom Reynolds brings more than 25 years of hands-on real estate experience to buyers, sellers, and investors throughout Door County and Northeast Wisconsin. His background spans every side of the industry — buying, selling, managing, renovating, and leasing residential and commercial properties. This breadth of experience allows Tom to deliver practical solutions to even the most complex real estate questions.

Proud to call Door County home, Tom is deeply familiar with the local market and its unique opportunities. In addition to being a seasoned real estate broker and skilled negotiator, Tom most recently served as Operations Manager for a short-term rental company, where he successfully managed more than 45 properties across the county. This expertise makes him a trusted advisor for clients interested in vacation homes and income-producing properties.

Tom's connection to Door County runs deep. His great-grandfather emigrated from Ireland and established a family farm just south of Jacksonport, raising a family of ten and laying roots that remain strong today. Tom spent his summers near the family homestead and has a lifelong appreciation for the area's natural beauty, vibrant communities, and recreational offerings.

Whether you're searching for a permanent residence, a vacation home, a short-term rental investment, or commercial property — or simply want guidance from a knowledgeable local — Tom is committed to providing honest advice and exceptional service every step of the way.

Million-Dollar Listings, Expert Historian

Tom has successfully represented buyers and sellers in some of Door County's most prestigious million-dollar-plus property transactions, from waterfront estates along the Lake Michigan shoreline to expansive private retreats in the heart of the peninsula. His understanding of the luxury market — from valuation to negotiation — sets him apart from the competition.

Beyond the numbers, Tom is a true historian of Door County. His knowledge of the area's history, architecture, and land use runs deep, giving clients a unique perspective on the properties they're buying or selling. He can tell you the story behind a century-old farmhouse, the history of a shoreline parcel, or the development patterns that shaped a neighborhood. This level of insight transforms a transaction into an experience.

Transparency breeds excellence. Tom believes that open, honest communication is the foundation of every successful real estate deal. No gimmicks, no pressure — just straightforward advice backed by decades of experience and a genuine love for Door County.
