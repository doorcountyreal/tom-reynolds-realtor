The Pride League Open: A Preview of Sunday's Chaos at Klein Creek

By Our Roaming Correspondent  ·  August 6, 2026

The Pride League is set to descend upon Klein Creek this Sunday for what organizers are calling "the most important round of golf since someone invented the mulligan." The stakes? Glory. The format? Degenerate. The participants? Questionable at best.

Here's your official preview of the Pride League Open — where legends are made, scores are fictional, and no one leaves without a new nickname.

🏌️ The Format: Skins by Day, Scramble by Night

The first 9 holes will be played as skins — meaning someone is going home poor and blaming the wind. The last 9 shift to a scramble, which is fancy code for "everyone picks up each other's garbage and we all pretend we shot 39."

There will be no official scorekeeper. There will be no official rules. There will only be vibes and disputes.

👥 The Players

⛈️ Hurricane Shawty

"Ole Shawty"

Arriving at Klein Creek like Aaron Rodgers returning to Lambeau — with a 5-minute warm-up drive and a delusional sense of confidence. Shawty has applied for a part-time job as a golf course marshal at Klein Creek, presumably so he can enforce the blistering pace of play he'll be violating by hole 3. He's spent the week studying the course like it's the ACT, which means he'll still three-putt from 12 feet. The Commissioner has been colluding with Shawty on fantasy rosters for years, and Sunday will be no different — expect some fruity tag-team energy on the fairways.

🥪 No Hogie Tribs

"The Negotiator"

Has somehow talked his way into another free round of golf — a feat more impressive than anything Tiger ever accomplished. Hogie will be unable to drop his usual 2 balls per hole or miscount by 4 strokes per side, as Shawty will be watching like a hawk. The good news? Hogie recently had a baby, which means he's running on 45 minutes of sleep and will be swinging out of spite. Expect him to negotiate at least 3 bogeys down to pars before the turn.

🦅 Da Peanut Cracka

"The American Icon"

After 15 years, this drunken nickname has stuck like old protein powder in a mixer bottle. Peanut Cracka will be representing the bald eagle on Sunday, which is fitting because he's about to soar, screech, and possibly eat trash out of a dumpster. He's also a new dad — meaning he and Hogie are tied for the Big Daddy Showdown belt. The question is: can the Cracka avoid moving slower than a hungover Scotty D after a long night with Hogie? Early reports say no. As kids we were jealous of Steve getting to eat Wendy's every day — now we're jealous he has an excuse to be off the course by hole 12 for a diaper change.

🧃 Young Juice Man

"The Returning Champion"

Making his long-awaited return to Klein Creek after traveling treacherous roads from Wisconsin. Juice Man is the defending fantasy champion and he's never let anyone forget it. He's coming to claim the one first and last title of the Pride League Open, and he's prepared to flood the group chat with scorecard photos that nobody asked for. His walk-up music will be whatever is loudest, and his trash talk will be relentless. He looks forward to the same energy he brings to fantasy — flooding your notifications with trades you'll never accept and blowing his golf fund like a bar tab in 2011.

🛠️ Scotty D

"The Diesel"

Scotty was contacted to pal up with the group, but Peanut Cracka said he's moving slower than a refrigerator full of expired leftovers. Sources say a long night with Hogie has left Scotty in a state that can only be described as post-Bochedda. If he shows up, expect a left-hand stroke that rivals his Chaturbate form and fantasy logic that sounds like it came straight from Matthew Berry's fever dream. With a clicker ability like that, it's a miracle he's still a bachelor.

🧢 The Commissioner

"Unavailable for Comment"

Could not be reached for comment. Sources say he's busy counting his Ozempic savings and working on his deepthroat technique for his next big stogie. The Commissioner's collusion with Shawty is well-documented — they've been drafting together like a fruity tag team for years. Expect him to show up late, blame traffic, and shoot exactly 2 strokes better than anyone expects while pretending he wasn't trying.

✦ ✦ ✦

📋 What's on the Line

The Name Game: The loser of the first 9 skins gets their fantasy name chosen by the winner. Hurricane Shawty and Peanut Cracka are on the chopping block — one bad approach shot and Ole Shawty becomes "The Three-Putt Bandit" for the rest of the season. The Cracka could end up as "Baby Formula Breath" or something worse knowing this group.

The Big Daddy Showdown: Both Hogie and Peanut Cracka have had babies this year. This round is officially ticketed as the Big Daddy Showdown — a battle of sleep-deprived fathers swinging for the fences on pure adrenaline and caffeine. The loser has to change the next diaper. Metaphorically. Probably.

Pride: Let's be real — nobody in this group has ever been good at golf. But they've all convinced themselves they're one round away from breaking 80. Sunday will not be that day. But it will be funny.

📜 Requirements for All Players

1. Best playlist. No one wants to hear your podcast phase. Crank the bangers.

2. Zingers. If you're not roasting someone's swing, are you even playing golf?

3. Lots of energy. This is a memorable round, not a funeral. Well, Hogie's scorecard might be.

4. No slow play. Shawty the Marshall will be watching. And he's got a clipboard.

"I've never seen someone negotiate their way out of more quadruple bogeys." — Chicago Taipei, on No Hogie Tribs

As the sun sets over Klein Creek on Sunday, one thing is certain: someone will be out $40 in skins, someone will have a new fantasy name they'll never live down, and everyone will head to the parking lot insisting they "actually played pretty well for not touching a club since last year."

Whether you're there in spirit or in the group chat lurking, this is the Pride League at its finest. May your drives be long, your putts be short, and your excuses be creative.
